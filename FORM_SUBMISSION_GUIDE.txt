 SAFECALL AI (సేఫ్ కాల్ AI) - STEP-BY-STEP SUBMISSION CHEAT SHEET

All your project files are right here in this folder on your Desktop!

------------------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 1: Open & Save the 1-Page Pitch as PDF
------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Double-click "safecall_pitch.html" to open it in Chrome / Edge / Brave.
2. Click the top button: "Print / Save as 1-Page PDF" (or press Ctrl + P).
3. Save it as "SafeCall_AI_Pitch.pdf" inside this folder.

------------------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 2: Upload to Google Drive (For Form Field 5)
------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Go to Google Drive (drive.google.com).
2. Right-click and choose "Upload Folder".
3. Select this folder: "SafeCall_AI_Submission" from your Desktop.
4. Once uploaded, right-click the folder in Google Drive -> "Share" -> change from "Restricted" to "Anyone with the link can view" -> click "Copy link".
5. Paste this link into Field 5 of the Microsoft Form!

------------------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 3: Record Your 2-Minute Demo Video (For Form Field 6)
------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Open PowerShell or Command Prompt, and run:
     cd OneDrive\Desktop\SafeCall_AI_Submission
     python app.py
2. Open your browser and go to:
     http://localhost:5000
3. Start recording your screen (using Loom, OBS, or press Win + Alt + R for Windows Game Bar recorder).
4. Follow the exact spoken script in "video_demo_script.md":
   - Show the header and say you built SafeCall AI for Gnani.ai challenge.
   - Click "Digital Arrest (Telugu)" to show the Tenglish transcript, high threat score, and Timbre voice warning.
   - Click "Digital Arrest (Hindi)" to show Hindi support.
   - Click "Amma Calling" to show it sleeps for saved family contacts to protect privacy.
5. Upload the video to Google Drive (or Loom / YouTube unlisted).
   - Set sharing to "Anyone with the link can view".
6. Paste the video link into Field 6 of the form!

------------------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 4: Post on LinkedIn (For Form Field 7 - Bonus Awards!)
------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Open "linkedin_post.txt".
2. Copy the text, replace [PASTE YOUR GITHUB OR GOOGLE DRIVE LINK HERE] with your Drive link, and [PASTE YOUR VIDEO LINK HERE] with your video link.
3. Post it on LinkedIn and make sure to tag @Gnani.ai.
4. Copy the link to your live LinkedIn post and paste it into Field 7 of the form!
