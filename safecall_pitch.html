01. The Problem: The ₹1,750+ Crore "Digital Arrest" Telephony Epidemic

Organized cyber syndicates systematically exploit Indian telephony to extort elderly citizens and students through Digital Arrest scams. Impersonating CBI, Mumbai Police, or Customs over phone, fraudsters intimidate victims in code-mixed languages: "Mee Aadhaar card meeda parcel lo MDMA drugs dorikayi... arrest warrant issue ayindi, phone cut cheyoddu!". Victims panic under isolation coercion. Existing solutions like Truecaller only screen numbers; once a spoofed call connects, victims have zero active defense. Standard speech models fail on noisy 8kHz GSM audio and code-mixed Tenglish/Hinglish.

02. The Solution: SafeCall AI (Privacy-First In-Ear Cyber Interceptor)

SafeCall AI is an on-device, sovereign voice intelligence companion that listens to incoming unknown calls in real time. It pipelines audio through Gnani.ai's sovereign multilingual stack to compute a live Coercion Threat Index and synthesizes a direct native-language emergency whisper into the victim's earpiece, breaking the scammer's psychological grip before funds can be transferred.

04. Privacy-by-Design Architecture (Zero Audio Storage)

• Whitelisted Contacts Bypass: Automatically hibernates for contacts saved in phonebook (family/friends).
• Ephemeral Volatile RAM Enclave: Audio chunks exist in memory for exactly 2 seconds and are immediately purged.
• Zero Audio Stored on Disk: No recordings or transcripts are ever persisted, ensuring 100% compliance with DPDP Act.
• On-Device Autonomous Pipeline: 100% automated model inference with zero human monitoring or third-party tracking.
